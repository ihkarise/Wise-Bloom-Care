## 1. Purpose
Specifies how the AI grounds its answers in the knowledge base via Retrieval-Augmented Generation, so responses are sourced, current, and non-fabricated. RAG is the mechanism that operationalises "evidence or nothing" (P6) for AI.

## 2. Scope
Indexing, retrieval, grounding, and citation of KB content for AI. KB structure: `101`; guardrails: `105`; pipeline: `100`.

## 3. Why RAG
The AI must not answer medical questions from parametric memory (which can be wrong/outdated). RAG restricts answers to the curated, sourced, versioned KB (`101`), enabling citations and preventing fabrication (`docs/02-Research/27` BR-4).

## 4. Indexing
- KB content items (`101`) are chunked and embedded into a vector index (plus keyword/metadata index for filters like life_stage, age/week, content_type).
- Each chunk retains its `source_ref`, `content_type`, `version`, and `last_reviewed`.
- Re-index on KB version changes; index version pinned per release (`docs/01-Product/16`).

## 5. Retrieval
- Given a query + authorised context (e.g., current gestational week/child age), retrieve top-k relevant chunks, filtered by life stage/age and content type.
- Prefer current, high-relevance, primary-source content; exclude deprecated versions.
- If retrieval yields insufficient relevant content → the AI returns "not enough information — consult your clinician" (no fabrication).

## 6. Grounding & Citation
- The generator is instructed (prompt, `102`) to use only retrieved content and cite `source_ref`s.
- Output carries the sources of the chunks used, resolving to `docs/02-Research/27`.
- Emergency content is never synthesised; only curated emergency items may be surfaced (as typed Emergency Warnings) and only via the curated set (`docs/02-Research/28` BR-4).

## 7. Guardrail Interaction
Post-generation, guardrails (`105`) verify the answer is grounded (no unsupported medical claims), correctly typed, sourced, and free of prohibited content; ungrounded claims are stripped or the answer is downgraded to "consult your clinician".

## 8. Privacy
- Retrieval uses minimal necessary user context; user PHI is not stored in the KB index.
- Any context sent to the provider is minimised (`100` §6).

## 9. Business Rules
- BR-1 AI medical answers are grounded in retrieved KB content; no parametric-only medical claims.
- BR-2 Retrieved chunks carry and propagate `source_ref` for citation.
- BR-3 Insufficient retrieval → safe "not enough info" response.
- BR-4 Index versioned with KB; pinned per release.
- BR-5 Emergency content is never generated by RAG; curated-only.

## 10. Edge Cases
Sparse/absent coverage (safe fallback); conflicting sources (prefer primary/current; may present balanced educational view with citations); stale index (re-index on KB change); adversarial/off-topic queries (filter + refuse via guardrails); multilingual retrieval (future).
