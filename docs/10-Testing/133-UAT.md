## 1. Purpose
Defines UAT — validation by representative real users that the product meets their needs and is acceptable to ship. Given the sensitive domain, UAT emphasises trust, calm, and the continuity experience, with careful, ethical handling of participants.

## 2. Scope
UAT participants, scenarios, acceptance criteria, and ethics. Internal testing: `130`–`132`.

## 3. Participants
- Representative of primary personas (`docs/03-UX/30`): expecting mothers, new mothers, and (if in scope) caregivers.
- Diversity across age, literacy, device, connectivity, and ability (incl. participants with disabilities for accessibility, `docs/03-UX/40`).
- **Ethical care:** given emotional sensitivity (pregnancy, potential loss), participants are volunteers, informed, able to withdraw; loss-path scenarios use synthetic personas, never a participant's real trauma.

## 4. UAT Scenarios (aligned to journeys `docs/03-UX/31`)
| Scenario | Validates |
|---|---|
| Onboard + start pregnancy | Ease, forgiving entry, reassurance (J1) |
| Weekly tracking | Logging speed, trend clarity, calm (J2) |
| Appointment + report | Reminder usefulness, report understanding (J3) |
| Delivery transition (synthetic) | Continuity — "it didn't make me start over" (J4) |
| Newborn care | Growth/milestone/vaccine usefulness; one-handed use (J5) |
| Caregiver (if in scope) | Sharing clarity + control (J7) |

## 5. Acceptance Criteria (UAT-level)
- Users can complete core journeys unaided.
- Users report the experience as calm/reassuring (M6 instrument, `docs/00-Vision/02` OQ-1).
- Users perceive **one continuous record** (no reset at delivery).
- Users can find and understand information; content types are clear.
- No trust-breaking or anxiety-raising moments in core flows.
- Accessibility participants can complete core flows.

## 6. Process
- Moderated sessions on staging with synthetic data; task-based; think-aloud.
- Findings triaged; blocking issues fixed before release (`docs/01-Product/16`).
- UAT is a release exit-gate input.

## 7. Business Rules
- BR-1 UAT covers primary-persona journeys incl. the delivery-transition continuity experience.
- BR-2 Participants are informed volunteers; loss scenarios are synthetic, never personal trauma.
- BR-3 Accessibility participants included where possible.
- BR-4 Blocking UAT findings fixed before release.
- BR-5 Synthetic data only; no real PHI.

## 8. Edge Cases
Low-literacy/low-connectivity participants; participants who have experienced loss (extra care; opt-out of loss scenarios); non-native-language users (future localisation); older devices.
