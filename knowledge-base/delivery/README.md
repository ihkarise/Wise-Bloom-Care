# Knowledge Base — Delivery

> **Educational information, not medical advice.** See `docs/02-Research/28-MEDICAL_DISCLAIMER.md`.

## Purpose
General education about labour, birth, and the delivery transition — supporting the delivery module (`docs/06-Modules/88-DELIVERY_MODULE.md`) and the timeline hinge (`docs/08-Timeline/111-DELIVERY_TRANSITION.md`). Educational only; the app never advises on delivery decisions (NG-3).

## Scope
- What the delivery event records (mode, gestational age at birth, birth metrics, Apgar) — educational context.
- Compassionate framing for outcomes, including pregnancy loss (handled without forcing a baby profile).

## Governance
- Typed + sourced; compassion-sensitive content reviewed carefully (`docs/10-Testing/132-MANUAL_TESTING.md`).
- Emergency red-flags live in `knowledge-base/emergency/` (curated), never inferred by the app.
- Specifics marked `[VERIFY]` confirmed against WHO/ACOG before display.
