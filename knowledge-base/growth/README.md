# Knowledge Base — Growth

> **Educational information, not medical advice.** See `docs/02-Research/28-MEDICAL_DISCLAIMER.md`.

## Purpose
Educational content about child growth on the WHO Child Growth Standards (0–60 months) — supporting the growth module (`docs/06-Modules/90-GROWTH_MODULE.md`). Explains percentiles/z-scores as context, never diagnosis.

## Scope
- What weight-for-age, length/height-for-age, weight-for-length, and BMI-for-age mean (educational).
- How to read a percentile/trend calmly; why ±2 SD is a reference, not a verdict (`docs/02-Research/25-WHO_CHILD_GROWTH.md`).
- Corrected age for preterm.

## Governance
- Typed + sourced; non-diagnostic; single measurements not over-interpreted (trend emphasised).
- Out-of-band framing routes to clinician-review, never emergency auto-escalation.
- WHO tables/method versioned with the dataset (`docs/05-Data/77-VERSIONING.md`).

## Sources
WHO Child Growth Standards (2006) and tools — see `docs/02-Research/25-WHO_CHILD_GROWTH.md`, `27-REFERENCES.md`.
