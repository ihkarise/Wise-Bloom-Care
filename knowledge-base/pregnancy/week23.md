# Pregnancy — Week 23

> **Educational information, not medical advice.** This content is general education about a typical pregnancy at week 23. Every pregnancy is different. It is not a diagnosis and does not replace your clinician. Discuss your pregnancy with your healthcare professional. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately. See `docs/02-Research/28-MEDICAL_DISCLAIMER.md`.

## Stage
Second trimester (pregnancy). Gestational age is dated from the last menstrual period (LMP) by common convention (`docs/02-Research/20-WHO_GUIDELINES.md`, `docs/06-Modules/82-PREGNANCY_MODULE.md`).

## What is often happening this week (educational)
[FACT / general] Rapid growth; lung development is ongoing. [VERIFY week-specific detail]

## Looking after yourself (educational, non-prescriptive)
- Attend your scheduled antenatal contacts; WHO recommends at least 8 contacts across pregnancy — your clinician owns your schedule (`docs/02-Research/20-WHO_GUIDELINES.md`). **[Clinical recommendation]**
- Nutrition, activity, and any supplements (e.g., folic acid) should follow your clinician's guidance (`docs/06-Modules/86-NUTRITION_MODULE.md`, `87-EXERCISE_MODULE.md`, `85-MEDICINES_MODULE.md`).
- Log your vitals and questions to bring to your next visit (`docs/06-Modules/83-VITALS_MODULE.md`).

## When to seek care
This app never decides whether something is an emergency. If you have warning symptoms (for example severe headache with vision changes, heavy bleeding, severe abdominal pain, or reduced fetal movements later in pregnancy), **seek urgent medical care** and contact your clinician. See the curated emergency guidance in `knowledge-base/emergency/`. **[Emergency warning — see curated set]**
