# Knowledge Base — Medicines

> **Educational information, not medical advice.** The app records and reminds; it never prescribes, doses, or recommends medicines (NG-2). See `docs/02-Research/28-MEDICAL_DISCLAIMER.md`.

## Purpose
General educational content about medicines/supplements a clinician may prescribe in pregnancy and early childhood — supporting the medicines module (`docs/06-Modules/85-MEDICINES_MODULE.md`). Strictly non-prescriptive.

## Scope
- Educational, sourced information about common clinician-prescribed supplements (e.g., what folic acid is and why it is commonly recommended in early pregnancy) — **informational only**, always deferring to the clinician.
- No dosing, no drug-interaction advice, no recommendations to start/stop anything.

## Governance
- Typed + sourced; strictly non-prescriptive; defers all decisions to the clinician.
- No dosing/interaction/clinical-judgement content (would violate NG-2).
- Specifics marked `[VERIFY]` confirmed against WHO/authoritative source before display.

## Sources
WHO ANC (supplementation context) — see `docs/02-Research/20-WHO_GUIDELINES.md`, `27-REFERENCES.md`. Any specific supplement education added must cite a primary source and be clinically reviewed.
